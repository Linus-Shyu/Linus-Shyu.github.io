Privacy Policy for luma bar

Last updated: September 30, 2026
App: luma bar (macOS) · Bundle ID com.lumabar.app
Developer: Linus Shyu

What this app is

luma bar is a third-party macOS utility. It shows music controls, optional lyrics, a light system glance, and an optional AI assistant. It is not an Apple system feature. If you buy the app, Apple processes the purchase. We do not receive your payment card details.

Data that stays on your Mac

  • Music controls, lyrics display, and system glance information are processed on your Mac.
  • Playback control talks to Apple Music and, if you use it, NetEase Cloud Music through Apple Events that you approve. We do not upload your listening history.
  • If you turn on Voice Whisper, the microphone is used only while you are recording, to turn speech into text on your Mac or through Apple’s speech recognition. We do not keep a copy of that audio.
  • Accessibility access, if you grant it, is used to read the frontmost app’s selected text when you ask for that feature. We do not monitor your keyboard in the background.

Folders you choose

Optional overlays (for example Cursor or Codex usage) read files only in folders you pick in the system open panel. Those grants are stored as security-scoped bookmarks on your Mac. We do not scan the rest of your disk, and we do not upload those files.

Optional AI assistant

The Agent is off unless you add your own API key (for example OpenAI or DeepSeek). That key is stored in your Mac Keychain. Prompts you type, and any text you choose to include, are sent to the provider you configure so it can answer. We do not operate that server, we do not see the key, and we do not use your prompts to train a model of our own.

Some optional lookups also contact public services from your Mac, such as weather (Open-Meteo) and lyric search (Apple’s iTunes Search and LRCLIB). Those requests contain the query needed to answer you, not an account profile.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use your content for third-party advertising, and the app does not include advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not ask for your contacts, and the Mac App Store build does not send Messages or capture the screen.

Children

luma bar is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date above will change and the new text will be published at this URL.

Contact

luma bar 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 9 月 30 日
开发者：Linus Shyu（许发鑫）

这是什么应用

luma bar 是一款第三方 macOS 工具，用来显示音乐控制、可选歌词、简要系统信息，以及一个可选的 AI 助手。它不是 Apple 系统功能。付费由 Apple 处理，我们拿不到你的支付卡信息。

留在本机的数据

  • 音乐界面、歌词和系统一览都在你的 Mac 上处理。
  • 播放控制通过你授权的 Apple Events 与 Apple Music 交互；如果你使用网易云音乐，也会与网易云音乐交互。我们不上传你的听歌记录。
  • Voice Whisper 只在你主动开始录音时使用麦克风，在本机或通过 Apple 的语音识别把语音转成文字。我们不保留这段音频。
  • 如果你授予辅助功能权限，它只用来在你调用相关功能时读取前台应用的选中文本。应用不会在后台监听键盘。

你选择的文件夹

可选的 Cursor / Codex 等用量显示，只会读取你在系统文件选择框里授权的文件夹。授权以安全书签保存在本机。我们不扫描其余磁盘，也不上传这些文件。

可选的 AI 助手

Agent 默认关闭。只有你自己填入 API 密钥（例如 OpenAI 或 DeepSeek）后才会启用。密钥保存在本机钥匙串。你输入的提示，以及你选择附带的文本，会发给你配置的那一家服务商以便得到回复。我们不运营那台服务器，看不到这把密钥，也不会用你的内容训练自己的模型。

部分可选查询会从你的 Mac 访问公开服务，例如天气（Open-Meteo）和歌词搜索（Apple iTunes Search、LRCLIB）。请求里只有完成查询所需的内容，没有账户档案。

我们不做的事

  • 不出售个人数据。
  • 不把你的内容用于第三方广告，应用里也没有广告或追踪 SDK。
  • 不读取通讯录。Mac App Store 版不发送信息，也不截取屏幕。

儿童

luma bar 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们也不会故意收集他们的个人信息。

变更

政策如有更新，会修改页面顶部的日期，并发布在这个网址。
