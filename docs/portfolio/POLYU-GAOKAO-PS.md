# PolyU Gaokao — optional English personal statement draft

Target programme: **JS3868 BSc (Hons) Scheme in Computing and AI**  
(Computer Science / Enterprise Information Systems)  
Official note: mainland Gaokao applicants usually need **no mandatory extra documents** for this scheme. Use this as optional upload / interview prep (~520 words).

---

My name is Faxin Xu (Linus Shyu). I am a Grade 12 student from Qinghai applying to The Hong Kong Polytechnic University through the mainland Gaokao route, for the BSc (Hons) Scheme in Computing and AI.

Competitive programming first taught me how to turn a hard problem into a precise algorithm. Building products then taught me something olympiad contests alone cannot: a system is unfinished until other people can use it. That is why PolyU’s application-oriented education feels like the right next step. I do not only want stronger theory; I want to keep shipping software that solves concrete problems.

Three projects show this loop most clearly.

For OI/ACM teammates, contest workflows were fragmented—opening problem sites, creating local files, calling models, and checking rating charts across different tools. I co-built **XTerminal**, a Python terminal workflow that connects these steps. It received a Third Prize on the Kimi For Vibe Coding track at AdventureX 2025 and is registered for software copyright. The project forced me to design for speed, reliability, and real contest habits rather than demo screenshots.

On macOS, I kept switching between music, reminders, system controls, and local AI agents. **Luma Bar** puts them into one native workspace around the MacBook notch. At AdventureX 2026 it won 2nd Place on the Amazon Web Services track, and the project is now open source. Shipping a native Swift app under hackathon pressure taught me interface constraints, local-agent integration, and how to cut features without killing usefulness.

**FeelFlow**, my SwiftUI emotional journal on the App Store, started from a simpler question: can AI support reflection without sending private notes to the cloud? Keeping data on-device forced careful product trade-offs between empathy features and privacy. Completing the App Store release mattered more to me than another prototype—because release is where engineering becomes responsibility.

I also built **CauseLink** (Third Prize, MoonShot48 Winter Hackathon 2026) to diagnose learning error causes rather than only collecting wrong answers, and **StarFetch**, a Rust system-info tool endorsed by the author of neofetch. Across these projects, the constant is the same: identify a real user need, finish an end-to-end system, and leave public evidence—code, demos, or a live product.

At PolyU I hope to deepen software engineering and applied AI inside Computing and AI, use experiential learning to test ideas against real environments, and grow toward work where systems are both technically sound and practically useful. My portfolio is at https://linusshyu.dev/portfolio/.
